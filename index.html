How to Become a JYP Trainee

Your step-by-step guide to chasing the idol dream ✨

🎤 What Is JYP Entertainment?

JYP Entertainment is one of South Korea’s top entertainment companies, home to artists like Stray Kids, TWICE, ITZY, NMIXX, and more.

📝 Basic Requirements

  • No nationality restrictions 🌍
  • No strict age limit (most trainees start young)
  • Strong skills in singing, dancing, rap, or visuals
  • Confidence, discipline, and passion

📹 How to Audition Online

  • Go to the official JYP audition website
  • Choose your category (Vocal / Dance / Rap / Model)
  • Prepare a short, clear video (1–2 minutes)
  • Use natural lighting and minimal editing
  • Submit and wait patiently

👉 Official site: audition.jype.com

💃 What JYP Looks For

  • Authenticity (be yourself)
  • Growth potential over perfection
  • Stage presence & facial expressions
  • Strong work ethic and attitude

⏳ After Passing

  • You may be contacted weeks or months later
  • You’ll train in singing, dancing, language, and performance
  • Training periods can last years
  • Debut is never guaranteed — but growth is

💌 Final Advice

Many idols were rejected multiple times before succeeding. Keep improving, stay healthy, and believe in yourself. If this is your dream — it’s worth chasing.
